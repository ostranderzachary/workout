# Forged

A mobile-friendly workout tracker. Log lifts and cardio, set daily goals, and see how close each muscle is to its goal on two body heat maps (lifting and cardio), by day, week, month, year or all time.

## Features

- **Quick tour:** the first time Forged opens, short cards explain goals, levels and title themes, boss fights and signing in, each pointing at where to find it (the You tab, or the level ring on Today). The sign-in card offers **Sign in** or **Not now**, and is left out for people already signed in. Skip it any time, and replay it from **You → Quick tour** or the Level screen (**Take the quick tour again**).
- **Choose a title theme:** right after the tour, a new visitor picks their title theme. Tapping a theme lists all 11 of its titles, from level 1 to 100 with their descriptions, at the bottom of the picker. It can't be closed until a theme is chosen. Anyone who already picked a theme isn't asked. Change it any time on the Level screen.

- **Log workouts:** the date, then exercises with reps and weight for each set (lb or kg). About 50 common exercises come with their muscles already mapped.
- **Exercise picker:** tap the exercise box to open a list, with no keyboard needed. The exercises you've logged most recently come first, then everything grouped by muscle: Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms, Legs and Core. A search box at the top narrows the list as you type. If your exercise isn't there, type its name and tap **Add "…" as a new exercise**, then tap the muscles it works. Forged remembers it and lists it under its muscle group from then on. **+ Add exercise** opens the picker straight away.
- **Heat map:** switch between Day, Week, Month, Year and All, and step back through earlier periods (see **Ranges** below). Tap a muscle to see which exercises worked it.
- **Sets per muscle:** a set counts 1 for the main muscles and 0.5 for helper muscles. The weekly view marks a 10–20 sets per week target.
- **Workout summary:** tapping a saved workout, in History, the calendar, the XP list or an exercise's progress page, opens a read-only summary. **Edit** opens the form, where you can change or delete it.
  - **Lifting:** sets, volume, XP and records at the top. Then each exercise with its muscles and sets, a **New record** or **First time** tag, and a comparison with the last time you did it, such as "+5 lb on your top set · last time Nov 17: 185 lb × 5 (2 sets)". Bodyweight exercises compare reps. Tap an exercise's name to open its progress page.
  - **Cardio:** time, distance (or stories), pace or speed, and XP, compared with your last session of the same activity, such as "+2m time · +0.5 mi · 51 sec/mi faster". Then the muscles it works.
  - **Both** end with how the workout's XP was earned.
- **Calendar:** the History tab opens with a month calendar, Monday to Sunday. Each day is shaded by the XP you earned, brighter for more, with a dot for lifting and a ring for cardio. Below it are the month's totals: workout days, lifting workouts, cardio sessions and XP. Tap a day to see its workouts and XP line items, tap a workout to open it, or log lifts or cardio for that day. Use the arrows (or swipe on a phone) to change month, and **Today** to jump back.
- **Logging a workout: the + button offers three choices.**
  - **Gym mode** (highlighted, "Lift now: plan your exercises, then log one set at a time") opens gym mode for today, starting at Plan. If you've already lifted today, it says "Lift now, carrying on with today's workout" with the sets so far, and that workout's sets show as done.
  - **Add or edit a past workout** opens the list form on yesterday (or on the day you're looking at in the Day view). If that day has a lifting workout, it opens so you can change it or add to it; change the date for another day. Tap **Start a separate workout** at the top of the form to log a new session instead.
  - **Cardio** opens the cardio form.
- **Changing the date in the list form:** if the new day already has a lifting workout, it opens, and anything you'd already entered is added to it. Editing a workout from History is different: changing its date moves it.
- Example workouts are shown until you log your first one. They are never saved.
- **Gym mode:** log as you train, in three steps shown at the top: **1 Plan › 2 Lift › 3 Save**. Start it with **+ → Gym mode**, **Start this session** on the suggestion card, or the gym mode box at the top of today's lifting form.
  - **1 Plan:** list the exercises you'll do and how many sets of each. **+ Add exercise** opens the exercise picker. Each exercise you've done before gets as many sets as last time (2–5) and the next step up, for example "Suggested: 3 × 10 at 125 lb". A new one gets 3 sets. Use **− / +** to change the sets, tap a name to swap the exercise, or **×** to remove it (with Undo). With an empty plan, **Today's suggestion** offers the whole session: tap **Use it**. **Start workout ›** is ready once there's an exercise. From the suggestion card, the plan is already filled in.
  - **2 Lift:** one exercise at a time, with its suggestion and last time's sets. Move between exercises with ‹ and ›, or by swiping left and right. The set you're on is shown large, with big − / + buttons for reps and weight and a **Done** button. After Done, the next set starts from what you just did: the same weight, and the same reps unless different reps were planned. Tap a done set to change it, or **Skip this set** (with Undo). When an exercise is finished, **Next: …** moves on, and after the last one **Review and save ›** appears.
  - **Rest timer:** ticking a set starts a 90-second rest. **−15s** and **+15s** adjust it, and the next rest starts from your new length. When it ends, it shows "Rest's up", beeps and vibrates. iPhones can't vibrate from a web page, so they only beep. **Timer sound** turns the beep off. Where the browser supports it, the screen stays on in gym mode.
  - **Progress strip:** for example "Set 7 of 14 · Lat Pulldown 2 of 5", with a bar across the top. On a phone it stays in view as you scroll.
  - **3 Save:** a review of what you ticked: sets done, exercises and XP from the sets, then each exercise with its sets. Planned sets you didn't do are listed as not saved. **Save workout** saves only the ticked sets and opens the workout summary.
  - **Moving around:** tap a step at the top, or **‹ Plan** and **Finish ›** at the bottom, to go back and forth; nothing is lost. Going back to Plan mid-workout shows each exercise's progress, and **Continue ›** returns you to where you were. Sets already saved in today's workout count as done.
  - **Edit as a list** (on the Plan step) switches to the list form. If you haven't ticked any sets yet, it becomes a plain form where every set you fill in is saved. If you have, the form marks done sets (✓), dims the rest and explains that only ticked sets are saved; **Save every set** switches to the plain form, and **Back to gym mode** returns you.
  - Gym mode is saved as a draft like the form, so a reload brings it back on the same step, with the sets you've ticked and the rest timer still running.
- **Nothing gets lost:**
  - **Drafts:** while the lifting or cardio form is open, your changes are saved on this device as you go. If the page reloads (iPhone Safari often does this when you switch apps), the form comes back with a note: "Restored the changes you hadn't saved, from 6:42 PM", and a **Discard** button. Drafts older than two days are dropped. Saving, deleting or closing the form clears the draft.
  - **Closed by accident:** closing a form with changes shows "Closed without saving your lifts" with **Undo**, which reopens it as it was. Closing a form you didn't change says nothing.
  - **Undo instead of "Are you sure?":** deleting a workout happens straight away, with **Undo** for 6 seconds, which saves it back exactly as it was. Removing an exercise, set or cardio activity from a form, and ending a boss fight early, offer Undo too.
  - **Swipe to remove:** on a phone, swipe a set row in the lifting form, or an entry in History, to the left to remove it (with Undo). A short swipe springs back, and scrolling up and down isn't affected.

## On your phone

The app is split into five sections: Today, Maps, Stats, History and You. On phones, the bar at the bottom holds Today, Maps, **+**, Stats and History, with **+** in the middle. On wider screens all five are in the left sidebar.

**The top bar:**
- **Flame** (left): the Forged mark, a forge flame in the heat-map greens. Tap it to go back to Today.
- **Crown:** your boss fights. It glows and shows a badge with the number waiting when a boss is ready.
- **Trophy:** the leaderboard (on the web; not in the Claude artifact version).
- **Level:** your level (and title on wider screens). Tap it for the Level screen: XP, titles and title theme.
- **Profile** (phones only; You is in the sidebar on wider screens): opens the You tab. It shows your initial once you're signed in. The sync status is a quiet line under your email on the You tab's Account row (red only when Forged can't reach your account), and in full on the Account screen.

- **Today:** the leaderboard card, the at-a-glance strip, your level, the get-started checklist, your progress summary and the suggestion for today.
- **Ranges:** Today is always about this week, so it has no range bar. Maps, Stats and History each have the Day / Week / Month / Year / All bar and ‹ › arrows at the top, and each remembers its own setting; for example, History can stay on Month while Maps stays on Week. Looking at an earlier range shows a **Back to this week** chip (or day, month or year) under the dates; tap it to return to now. In History, changing the range also moves the calendar to that month. Tapping a muscle under "Most behind" on Today opens the map for this week. Saving a workout while on Maps, Stats or History moves that tab to the workout's date; Today stays on this week.
- **Maps:** the lifting heat map.
- **Stats:** cardio gauges, lifting goal bars and the cardio breakdown.
- **History:** your workout list.
- **You:** your profile and every setting in one place (see below).

The **+** button in the middle logs a workout: **Gym mode**, **Add or edit a past workout** (the list form) or **Cardio**. On phones, forms open full screen and fit the space above the keyboard. The Save button stays pinned at the bottom, and the field you're typing in scrolls into view. Number fields have **− / +** buttons, so you rarely need the keyboard. Each tap changes reps by 1, weight by 5 lb (2.5 kg), cardio minutes by 5 and distance by 0.5. Tapping + on an empty set starts from the set above, or from what you did last time for that exercise. New sets copy the set above.

Wider screens put all five in a left sidebar, with a **Log workout** button at the top. Each tab lays its panels out in two columns, and the Maps tab uses the full width.

## Today at a glance

A strip just below the leaderboard card shows where your week stands:

- **Level ring:** your level, with the ring filling as you earn XP toward the next one. Tap it for the Level screen.
- **Title and XP:** your title and "70 / 300 XP to level 4 · Gold Cloak at 10". Tap for the Level screen. (Today no longer has a separate level card: your level shows in the header chip and this ring.)
- **This week:** seven dots, Monday to Sunday, filled for days you trained. Today is outlined and the days ahead are dashed. Below them: "2 of 4 days this week" (or the days trained, with no days goal) and your streak.
- **What's at stake:** for example "Train 2 more days by Sunday (3 days left, including today) for +50 XP and your streak. Falling short costs 80 XP", or "Days goal met: +50 XP", followed by last week's result. When the red streak warning below is showing, this line keeps only the cost.
- **Urgent lines,** shown only when they apply:
  - **Boss fight:** a running fight with what's left and the time left, for example "Road Wraith · 3.2 of 6.2 mi · 21h 40m left". Tap to open it.
  - **Streak:** when your streak breaks unless you train every day left this week and you haven't trained today, for example "Train today and every day through Sunday to keep your 2-week streak". Tap to log a workout.
- **Next:** the suggestion in one line, for example "Pull · about 10 sets · +140 XP". Tap to jump to the full suggestion (it opens if folded). Hidden when you've hidden the suggestion for today.

**Fold cards to one line:** the boss card, Your progress and the suggestion each have a small ⌃ button that folds them to a single line, such as "Your progress · 4 of 15 muscle goals · 60% of cardio · Nov 16–22". Tap the line to open the card again. Forged remembers which cards you folded (across devices when you're signed in), and the order stays the same.

## The You tab

Everything about you, and every setting, in one place.

- **Profile:** your level, title and XP (tap for the Level screen), then rows for **Level and titles**, **Title theme** (opens the Level screen at the themes), **Bosses** (says which boss is ready or how a fight is going, with a badge; the You tab itself shows the badge and glows while a boss waits), **Leaderboard** (on the web), **Goals** (a one-line summary, such as "4 days · 113 sets · 2h 30m of cardio a week") and **Share progress**.
- **Settings:**
  - **Weight unit** (lb or kg) and **Distance unit** (mi or km), applied everywhere. The lifting and cardio forms still have their own unit switches.
  - **Rest timer:** − / + changes gym mode's rest length in 15-second steps (15 seconds to 10 minutes). **Timer sound** turns the beep on or off.
  - **Body weight:** your latest weigh-in; tap to record a new one.
  - **Account** or **Sign in** (on the web), with your email and sync status.
  - **Backup and restore** and **Quick tour**.

## Goals

Open **Goals** from the You tab, or **Change goals** under Your progress on Today.

**Three quick questions:** the first time you set goals (from the Get started checklist or Goals), Forged asks how many days a week you train (2–6), your focus (balanced, upper body, lower body or cardio first) and how experienced you are (new, some experience, experienced). It then fills in every goal and shows a summary: days, total sets and cardio a week, and each muscle's sets. **Use these goals** saves them; **Fine-tune** opens the full form with them filled in, not yet saved. Each muscle keeps its usual share (big muscles most): experience sets the base (8, 12 or 16 sets a week for a big muscle at 4 days), fewer days scale it down and more days up, and the focus moves sets between upper and lower body (cardio first lightens lifting and sets 4 hours of cardio). Distance and stairs goals stay as they were. The full form offers the questions again at the top, and they offer **Use the full form** on the first question.

**Change one goal where you see it:** tap the pencil next to a muscle's bar on Stats, **Change goal** on a muscle selected on the map, the cardio, distance or stairs goal in the Stats cardio panel, or "2 of 4 days this week" in Today's at-a-glance strip. A small screen explains the goal, shows how you're doing this week, and has − / + and **Save**. **All goals** opens the full form.

All goal changes are saved with the date, so earlier weeks are still judged by the goals that applied then.

**The full form** sets every weekly target: workout days, sets per week for each muscle, and cardio minutes, distance and stairs. The defaults are 4 days, 10 sets a week for big muscles, 5–8 for smaller ones, 150 minutes of cardio and 10 miles.

- **Summary at the top**, updating as you change numbers: "4 days · 113 sets · 2h 30m cardio a week", then about how many sets a workout that takes. Since one set of an exercise counts toward about two muscles on average (the main ones fully, helpers by half), 113 goal sets over 4 days is about 15 sets a workout. If it's more than 25, it suggests more days or fewer sets.
- **− / + on every goal**, or type a number.
- **This week so far** next to each goal, such as "7 done" or "3 so far" for days. It turns green once the goal is met, and updates as you change the goal.
- **How goals work** opens a short explanation. **Start over** (or "Not sure where to start?" before you've saved goals) offers the 3 quick questions. **Reset to defaults** is a link at the end; it fills the defaults in without saving.
- The bottom bar is just **Cancel** and **Save goals**.

Other ranges scale by their number of days. A day's target is a seventh of the weekly goal, and a 31-day month is 31/7 of it. A year uses 365 or 366 days, and all time counts from your first workout to today.

Each muscle is coloured by how close it is to its target: four shades for each quarter of the way, and the darkest once the goal is met. Set a goal to 0 to leave that muscle off the map.

**Cardio breakdown:** for the selected range, each activity shows sessions, time, distance, and average pace (min/mi or min/km) for runs, walks and hikes, or speed for rides. Highlight cards show your longest session and best pace or fastest speed for each activity. Each session in the workout list also shows its pace or speed. Pace and speed use only sessions logged with a distance.

## XP, levels and titles

Every workout earns XP:

- **Lifting:** 25 XP per workout plus 10–15 XP per set, counting up to 40 sets. Heavier sets earn a little more on a gentle curve: bodyweight is 10, 45 lb is 12, 135 lb is 13, 225 lb is 14, and 400 lb or more is 15. A 15-set workout at 135 lb earns 220 XP and one at 315 lb earns 235, so lighter lifters aren't left far behind.
- **Cardio:** 25 XP per session plus 2 XP per minute, counting up to 180 minutes.

Each level needs 100 XP more than the one before. Level 1 to 2 takes 100 XP, 9 to 10 takes 900 XP, and level 10 is 4,500 XP in total.

Every 10 levels unlocks a new title. Pick a **title theme** on the Level screen (or **You → Title theme**): Game of Thrones (default), Warriors, Greek mythology, Norse mythology, Egyptian mythology, Red Rising (Reaper at level 100), Disney, Solo Leveling (Shadow Monarch at level 100), Star Wars, Dragon Ball, Marvel heroes (Phoenix at level 100), Marvel villains (Thanos at level 100), DC heroes (Superman at level 100), DC villains (Darkseid at level 100), Hunter x Hunter (Meruem at level 100), Attack on Titan (Levi Ackerman at level 100), Naruto (Naruto Uzumaki at level 100), military ranks for each branch (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force and Coast Guard), Famous warriors (legendary fighters, with Miyamoto Musashi at level 100), Martial artists (proven fighters, with Khabib Nurmagomedov at level 100), Immortal Great Souls (the ranks from Phil Tucker's series, with Scorio at level 100) or Martial arts belts. The picker groups them under headings: Fantasy and sci-fi, Mythology, Anime, Comics, Military, and Warriors and martial arts. Each theme has 11 titles from a humble start at level 1 to the top at level 100, each with a short description. Picking a theme there scrolls straight to its titles. Switching themes only changes the names; your level and XP stay the same. The theme is saved on your device.

### Weekly bonuses and penalties

Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Each week is judged by the goals in effect that week, so changing a goal doesn't rewrite past weeks.

| Event | XP |
|---|---|
| Each muscle's weekly sets goal met | +20 |
| Every muscle goal met | +100 |
| Weekly cardio time goal met | +75 |
| Weekly distance goal met | +75 |
| Workout-days goal met (set in Goals, default 4 days) | +50 |
| Streak: days goal met several weeks in a row | +20 per extra week, up to +200 |
| Each workout day missed in a finished week | −40, and the streak resets |

Your first week is never penalised. Total XP never goes below 0, but penalties can take you down a level.

The at-a-glance strip on Today shows this week's workout days, what you'll earn by hitting your goal, what you'll lose if you fall short (days left always count today), and last week's result.

The **History** tab has an XP chart for the selected range:

- one bar per day for a week or month, per week for a year, and per month beyond that
- workout XP in green, weekly bonuses stacked on top, and missed-day penalties below the zero line
- your total for the range and your best day or week

Tap a bar to list where its XP came from: today's bar is listed to start with. The list shows:

- each workout, broken down into the 25 XP for logging it and each exercise's sets, or the cardio minutes. Notes appear when the 40-set or 180-minute caps apply.
- each weekly bonus, boss reward and missed-day penalty
- a total that matches the bar

Tap a workout in the list to open it. This week's bonuses appear on today's bar until the week ends.

Saving a workout shows the XP earned. Reaching a new level or title brings up a celebration. Tap your level (the chip at the top, or your title in the at-a-glance strip) to see the title ladder and recent XP.

XP is worked out from your logged workouts, so editing or deleting a workout updates it. Example workouts don't earn XP.

## Body weight

Record your body weight from **You → Body weight**, the **Update weight** button on the Stats weight chart, or the Get started checklist: type it in or use the − / + buttons (1 lb or 0.5 kg per tap), then **Save**. It's saved with today's date, and the screen lists your last five weigh-ins.

- Changing it on a later day adds a new dated entry, so you build a history. Changing it again the same day updates that day's entry.
- Saving without changing the number doesn't add an entry.
- It isn't used for XP or goals.
- The **Stats** tab shows your current weight, the change over the last 30 days and since your first entry, and a line chart of every weigh-in. Tap a point to see its date and weight.
- "Record your body weight" is a step in the Get started checklist.

## Personal records

Forged keeps the heaviest set you've logged for each exercise. kg and lb are compared correctly.

- **While logging:** your best shows under the exercise name, for example "Your best: 185 lb × 5 · Oct 20".
- **When you beat it:** the save message says so, for example "New record: Bench Press 190 lb". The first time you log an exercise sets its record without announcing one.
- **On the Stats tab:** all your records are listed, newest first, including bodyweight exercises such as pull-ups (most reps in a set).

## Exercise progress

Tap an exercise in your records, or **See progress** under an exercise while logging, to open its progress page:

- **Summary:** your best set, estimated one-rep max, last time, and how your top set has changed since about a month ago.
- **Chart:** every session, switchable between top weight, estimated one-rep max (weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30), from sets of 1–12 reps) and volume. Bodyweight exercises chart most reps and total reps instead. Tap a point to see that session, and the best is highlighted.
- **Every session, newest first:** each with its sets and volume. Tap one to open that workout. Opened from the logging form, the page sits on top of it, so nothing you've entered is lost.

## Sharing your progress

Tap **Share progress** on the You tab, or on the Level screen, to make two share images for this week. Swipe between them on a phone; wider screens show them side by side.

1. **Overview:** your level, title, XP bar and next title; this week's workouts, workout days, streak and total XP; the lifting heat map with your muscle goals (closest to done first) and any records you beat this week; and the title ladder.
2. **Gauges:** the cardio rings for time, distance and stairs; the four lifting gauges (sets, muscle goals, volume against last week, and push / pull); and your boss: the fight in progress, the boss waiting, or the next one to unlock. Each gauge shows whether you're on pace, and the white dot marks today's even-pace point.

**Share** sends both images through your phone's share menu. Where a browser can't share images, they're saved to your downloads instead. **Save images** saves both.

Everything is generated on your device; nothing is uploaded.

## Boss fights

Every 5 levels a **boss** appears. When you're ready, tap **Start fight**: you then have 24 hours, and every workout you log during the fight counts toward the challenge. The boss falls the moment you reach it. If time runs out, the boss stays and you can start again. You can also end a fight early.

Four types take turns, and each comes back harder:

| Type | Levels | Challenge within 24 hours |
|---|---|---|
| Runner | 5, 25, 45 ... | 10K, 15K, half marathon, 30K, marathon, 50K |
| Stairs | 10, 30, 50 ... | 150, 200, 275, 350, 425, 500 stories |
| Iron | 15, 35, 55 ... | 40, 45, 50, 55, 60, 70 lifting sets |
| Volume | 20, 40, 60 ... | 25k, 30k, 35k, 45k, 55k, 65k lb lifted |

Each reward equals the XP of the next two levels, plus 5% more for every boss before it. Rewards keep pace with the steeper level costs and keep growing: about 1,100 XP at level 5, 2,200 at 10, 14,650 at 50 and 39,200 at 100.

A boss beaten later on the same day it appeared counts too.

A new boss is announced with a popup. The **crown** at the top shows a badge and glows while one is waiting, and the You tab's **Bosses** row says which (also **Bosses** in the desktop sidebar). The Today tab shows the boss with a **Start fight** button, and during a fight, the time left and your progress.

## Leaderboard

A global leaderboard for signed-in users, with four tabs:

- **This week** and **This month:** all XP earned in the current week (Monday to Sunday) or month, the same total as the History page: workouts, weekly bonuses and boss rewards, minus any missed-day penalties. A countdown shows when the board resets.
- **Total XP:** all-time XP, including weekly bonuses and boss rewards.
- **Bosses:** bosses defeated.

**Each row shows:**
- the rank, with gold, silver and bronze for the top three
- the display name, title and title theme
- the level
- the score
- the workouts in that period, the weekly streak and bosses defeated

Your own row is highlighted, even if you're outside the top 100, and the top of the board shows your rank, for example "#2 of 42 players this week".

**Joining is opt-in.** Pick a display name (2–24 characters, unique). Others only see your name, title, level and scores, never your email or workouts. You can change the name or leave any time. Your entry updates itself as you train.

**Finding it:** a card on the Today tab shows your weekly rank. There's also the **trophy** at the top, a **Leaderboard** row on the You tab and **Leaderboard** in the desktop sidebar. The Claude artifact version doesn't have a leaderboard, because it doesn't use Supabase.

The app reports your XP, matching what you see in Forged, and the database checks every number against what your logged workouts, weekly bonuses and boss fights in that period could earn. Anything above that is capped, and the level is recalculated, so the numbers can't be inflated by editing the app. The setup is in [`supabase/leaderboard.sql`](supabase/leaderboard.sql), run once in the SQL Editor after `schema.sql`.

## Suggestion for today

The Today tab shows an idea for what to train, worked out from your week so far. It's only a suggestion, so train whatever feels right.

- **The focus:** Push, Pull, Legs or Core. It's the group with the most sets still to go toward this week's goals, counting only muscles that have had at least two days' rest. A lift counts as training a muscle whether it's a main or a helper muscle.
- **Late in the week** (Saturday and Sunday), it favours the goals closest to being met instead: the group with the most muscles 5 sets or less from their goal. Those are the ones a session can still finish, and each finished goal is worth XP. The card says so when this applies.
- **Why:** each of those muscles with its sets to go and when it was last trained, for example "Lats: 8 sets to go · last trained 6 days ago".
- **A session you could do:** for those muscles, the exercises you did most recently, or a common pick for one you haven't trained. A muscle that needs more than 5 sets gets a second exercise. Tap a name to see its progress.
- **How many sets:** the sets still to go are spread across the exercises, 2–5 sets each. Helper muscles count half, so a Lat Pulldown also counts toward biceps. The session is kept to about 18 sets; anything over that is left for another day, and the card says roughly how much.
- **The next step up,** worked out from last time:
  - Every set at your top weight hit its reps: about 5 lb more (2.5 lb under 40 lb; 2.5 kg or 1 kg in kilograms). For example "Back Squat 4 × 5 at 230 lb · Up 5 lb: you hit every rep at 225 lb".
  - Reps dropped off: the same weight, aiming for the full reps on every set.
  - Bodyweight exercises: one more rep than your best.
  - Exercises new to you: a set count only, with a tip to pick a weight you can manage for 8–12 reps.
- **Start this session:** opens gym mode on its Plan step with those exercises and set counts filled in, each marked "Suggested:" with the reason (the note isn't saved). Change anything, then **Start workout**. If you've already lifted today, they're added to that workout. Prefer one form? **Edit as a list** on the Plan step switches to it, and every set you fill in there is saved. A suggested exercise left without reps is skipped.
- **XP within reach:** the parts of the game today's workout can move forward.
  - **A running boss fight:** what's left, the time left and the reward, for example "Boss fight: Road Wraith. 3.2 mi to go, 21h 40m left. Win for +1,100 XP", with a **Log a run** or **Log stairs** button. For a sets boss it says how many sets the suggested session adds.
  - **Workout days:** if you haven't trained today and are short of your days goal, what training today earns, for example "Training today reaches your 4-day goal for +50 XP and a 2-week streak (+20 XP)". If the goal is out of reach, it says each day you train still saves 40 XP in missed-day penalties.
  - **Muscle goals:** which goals the suggested session would finish, for example "This session would meet the lats and biceps goals for +40 XP", plus the +100 XP bonus when it would finish every muscle goal.
- **Other ideas** switch to another focus. A cardio option appears when you're behind on cardio time or distance, with the +75 XP each goal earns when it's met.
- **If every muscle goal is met,** or everything behind was trained in the last two days, it suggests a rest day instead.
- **Hide for today** hides the card until tomorrow.

## Understanding your progress

- **Get started checklist:** walks new users through setting goals, logging lifts and logging cardio. Each step ticks off when done. You can hide it.
- **Your progress:** shows how many muscle goals are met, how much of the cardio goal is done, the days left in the range, and the muscles furthest behind. Tap one to see it on the map.
- **Pace:** in the current week, month or year, each goal is marked *On pace*, *Behind pace* or *Goal met*. The comparison is with how far through the range you are; for example, 2 of 7 days in, you'd be on pace with 2/7 of the goal. The thin line on each bar marks that point. Past ranges show *Goal met* or *Missed*.
- **Legend:** each map labels its colour steps (none yet, under 25%, 25–49%, 50–74%, 75–99%, goal met). "How to read this map" explains how sets and minutes are counted.

## Cardio

The **Stats** tab also has a **Lifting** panel with four gauges:

- **Sets:** sets counted toward your muscle goals, out of your total weekly goal. Helper muscles count half, and sets beyond a muscle's goal don't add more.
- **Muscle goals:** a ring with one segment per muscle, coloured like the heat map. Tap a segment to see that muscle's numbers.
- **Volume:** total reps × weight against the previous week, month or year, with a note when you're up on last time.
- **Push / pull:** a dial comparing push sets (chest, shoulders, triceps) with pull sets (lats, traps, biceps). 40–60% push counts as balanced.

**Stairs** sessions are logged in stories climbed (− / + in steps of 5) instead of distance.

Tap **+ → Cardio** to log cardio: the date, then one or more activities, each with its type, minutes, optional distance (mi or km), or stories for stairs, and an optional name. Tap **+ Add activity** to log several at once, such as a run and a stair climb. Each activity is saved as its own session. If the day already has cardio, the form opens with that day's sessions and a new activity ready to fill in. You can change or remove any of them (✕) and save. The menu shows "Continue today's cardio" with the activities so far. Picking another date in the form, or using the Day view on an earlier date, does the same for that day. Editing a session from History changes just that session, and a new date moves it. Each activity maps to the muscles it works. For example, a cycle works quads fully and calves, hamstrings and glutes partly. Cardio progress shows as three doughnut gauges, **Time**, **Distance** and **Stairs**, each filling toward your weekly goal for the selected range (default 150 minutes, 10 miles and 50 stories a week, set in Goals). A white dot on each ring marks where you'd be today at an even pace.

## Running it

It's a single static file with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host it on any static host. For GitHub Pages: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root**.

Without an account, workouts are saved in the browser's `localStorage`, so they stay on that device and browser only. Sign in to sync them (below).

## Accounts and sync

On the You tab, tap **Sign in**. Sign in with **Google**, or with your email: Forged emails you a sign-in link, and tapping it opens Forged signed in. Open the email on the device you want to sign in on. If you use Forged from your home screen, sign in with Google, because email links open in the browser instead. If the email ever contains a code (see the setup notes), tap **Email has a code instead?** and type it in.

**Profile:** once you're signed in, the Account screen (You → Account) has a **Profile** section:

- **Signed in with:** Google or Email link, so you know which to use on your other devices.
- **Display name:** the name others see on the leaderboard. **Change** edits it in place (Enter saves). It's the same name as on the Leaderboard screen, so changing it in either place changes both.
- **Show me on the leaderboard:** a switch. Turning it on asks for a display name (suggested from your email) and joins the board. Turning it off asks first, then removes your entry; you can rejoin any time.
- **How others see you this week:** a preview of your leaderboard row, with your name, title and theme, level, workouts this week and this week's XP. It follows the name as you type. Before you join, it shows "How you'd appear".
- The You tab's Account row shows your display name too.

Once you're signed in:

- **What's stored in your account and synced to every device you sign in on:**
  - workouts, exactly as logged (custom exercises come with them)
  - goals, with their dated history, body weight entries and boss fights
  - settings: title theme, weight and distance units, last cardio activity, rest timer length and sound, folded Today cards, each tab's range, whether you've done the quick tour, picked a title theme or hidden the Get started checklist, which bosses have already been announced, and a suggestion hidden for today
  - your leaderboard entry, if you've joined
- **Not synced, on purpose:** the tab you were last on, and an unsaved form (each device keeps its own draft).
- When a device that has its own settings signs in, the account's settings win, except that anything done on either device stays done (the tour, the theme pick, a hidden checklist) and bosses announced on either aren't announced again.
- An open app picks up changes from another device within a few seconds, or when you switch back to it.
- Everything is also kept on the device. If you log a workout with no signal, it's saved and sent when you're back online. The You tab's Account row shows the sync status (red only when Forged can't reach your account); the Account screen has the details.
- **Data from before you signed in:**
  - **Goals:** if the account has none yet, this browser's goals (with their history, body weight entries and boss fights) and settings are added automatically.
  - **Workouts:** Forged offers to add them, since a shared browser might hold someone else's.
  - **Extra weigh-ins and boss fights:** if the account already has goals, any of these this browser has that the account doesn't are offered too, for example "This browser has 2 workouts, 1 weigh-in and 1 boss fight from before you signed in". **Add them to my account** merges them, and nothing already in the account is added twice.
- **Sign out** removes the account's copy from the device, and this browser's own workouts are shown again. If changes haven't synced yet, Forged warns you first.

Accounts use [Supabase](https://supabase.com) (sign-in and a Postgres database). The database's row-level security rules mean each person can only read and change their own rows, so the public key in the page is safe to share. The Claude artifact version keeps using its own built-in storage.

### Setting up Supabase

1. Create a Supabase project. Turn on the **Email** and **Google** sign-in providers, and set the **Site URL** and **Redirect URLs** to the GitHub Pages address.
2. The standard Supabase sign-in email works as it is. Optional: once you've set up custom SMTP (Supabase only lets you edit email templates with it), you can add `Your Forged code: {{ .Token }}` to the **Magic Link** template, so the email also includes a code.
3. Open the **SQL Editor**, paste [`supabase/schema.sql`](supabase/schema.sql) and tap **Run**. It creates the `workouts` and `profiles` tables, the security rules and live updates, and is safe to run again.
4. The project URL and publishable key are set at the top of the storage code in `index.html` (`const SB`).

## Backup and restore

On the You tab, **Backup and restore → Export backup** saves a single `.json` file with your workouts, goals (including their history), body weight entries and settings (title theme, units, rest timer, folded cards, each tab's range and the other synced settings).

On another phone or browser, choose **Import backup** and pick that file. Forged shows what's in it, then lets you choose:

- **Merge into this device:** adds the backup's workouts and weigh-ins and keeps what's already there. The same workout is never added twice, and this device's goals and settings stay as they are.
- **Replace everything:** makes this device match the backup exactly, including goals and settings.

Nothing changes until you pick one.
