# Forged

A mobile-friendly workout tracker. Log lifts and cardio, set daily goals, and see how close each muscle is to its goal on two body heat maps (lifting and cardio), by day, week, month, year or all time.

## Features

- **Quick tour:** the first time Forged opens, short cards explain goals, levels and title themes, boss fights and signing in, each pointing at the button it describes. The sign-in card offers **Sign in** or **Not now**, and is left out for people already signed in. Skip it any time, and replay it from the Level screen (**Take the quick tour again**).
- **Choose a title theme:** right after the tour, a new visitor picks their title theme. Tapping a theme lists all 11 of its titles, from level 1 to 100 with their descriptions, at the bottom of the picker. It can't be closed until a theme is chosen. Anyone who already picked a theme isn't asked. Change it any time on the Level screen.

- **Log workouts:** the date, then exercises with reps and weight for each set (lb or kg). About 50 common exercises come with their muscles already mapped.
- **Exercise picker:** tap the exercise box to open a list, with no keyboard needed. The exercises you've logged most recently come first, then everything grouped by muscle: Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms, Legs and Core. A search box at the top narrows the list as you type. If your exercise isn't there, type its name and tap **Add "…" as a new exercise**, then tap the muscles it works. Forged remembers it and lists it under its muscle group from then on. **+ Add exercise** opens the picker straight away.
- **Heat map:** switch between Day, Week, Month, Year and All, and step back through earlier periods. Tap a muscle to see which exercises worked it.
- **Sets per muscle:** a set counts 1 for the main muscles and 0.5 for helper muscles. The weekly view marks a 10–20 sets per week target.
- **History:** tap a workout to edit or delete it.
- **Keep adding to a day's workout:** if you've already logged lifts today, **+ → Lifting** reopens that workout (the latest one, if there are several) at its last exercise, so you can add sets or exercises and save. The menu shows "Continue today's workout" with the sets so far. In the Day view on an earlier date, it offers that day's workout instead. Tap **Start a separate workout** at the top of the form to log a new session.
- **Logging lifts for an earlier day:** pick a date in a new lifting form, and if that day already has a lifting workout, it opens. Anything you'd already entered is added to it. Editing a workout from History is different: changing its date moves it.
- Example workouts are shown until you log your first one. They are never saved.

## On your phone

The app is split into four tabs: on a bar at the bottom on phones, and in a sidebar on wider screens.

- **Today:** your level, the get-started checklist and your progress summary.
- **Maps:** the lifting heat map.
- **Stats:** cardio gauges, lifting goal bars and the cardio breakdown.
- **History:** your workout list.

The **+** button in the middle logs lifts or cardio. On phones, forms open full screen and fit the space above the keyboard. The Save button stays pinned at the bottom, and the field you're typing in scrolls into view. Number fields have **− / +** buttons, so you rarely need the keyboard. Each tap changes reps by 1, weight by 5 lb (2.5 kg), cardio minutes by 5 and distance by 0.5. Tapping + on an empty set starts from the set above, or from what you did last time for that exercise. New sets copy the set above.

Wider screens use the same four tabs in a left sidebar, with a **Log workout** button at the top. Each tab lays its panels out in two columns, and the Maps tab uses the full width.

## Goals

Tap **Goals** to set a weekly target: sets per week for each muscle group, and minutes per week for cardio. The defaults match the Standard preset: 10 sets a week for big muscles, 5–8 for smaller ones, 150 minutes of cardio and 10 miles.

Other ranges scale by their number of days. A day's target is a seventh of the weekly goal, and a 31-day month is 31/7 of it. A year uses 365 or 366 days, and all time counts from your first workout to today.

Each muscle is coloured by how close it is to its target: four shades for each quarter of the way, and the darkest once the goal is met. Set a goal to 0 to leave that muscle off the map.

**Cardio breakdown:** for the selected range, each activity shows sessions, time, distance, and average pace (min/mi or min/km) for runs, walks and hikes, or speed for rides. Highlight cards show your longest session and best pace or fastest speed for each activity. Each session in the workout list also shows its pace or speed. Pace and speed use only sessions logged with a distance.

## XP, levels and titles

Every workout earns XP:

- **Lifting:** 25 XP per workout plus 10–15 XP per set, counting up to 40 sets. Heavier sets earn a little more on a gentle curve: bodyweight is 10, 45 lb is 12, 135 lb is 13, 225 lb is 14, and 400 lb or more is 15. A 15-set workout at 135 lb earns 220 XP and one at 315 lb earns 235, so lighter lifters aren't left far behind.
- **Cardio:** 25 XP per session plus 2 XP per minute, counting up to 180 minutes.

Each level needs 100 XP more than the one before. Level 1 to 2 takes 100 XP, 9 to 10 takes 900 XP, and level 10 is 4,500 XP in total.

Every 10 levels unlocks a new title. Pick a **title theme** on the Level screen: Game of Thrones (default), Warriors, Greek mythology, Norse mythology, Egyptian mythology, Red Rising (Reaper at level 100), Disney, Solo Leveling (Shadow Monarch at level 100), Star Wars, Dragon Ball, Marvel heroes (Phoenix at level 100), Marvel villains (Thanos at level 100), DC heroes (Superman at level 100), DC villains (Darkseid at level 100), Hunter x Hunter (Meruem at level 100), Attack on Titan (Levi Ackerman at level 100), Naruto (Naruto Uzumaki at level 100), military ranks for each branch (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force and Coast Guard) or Martial arts belts. The picker groups them under headings: Fantasy and sci-fi, Mythology, Anime, Comics, Military, and Warriors and martial arts. Each theme has 11 titles from a humble start at level 1 to the top at level 100, each with a short description. Picking a theme there scrolls straight to its titles. Switching themes only changes the names; your level and XP stay the same. The theme is saved on your device.

### Weekly bonuses and penalties

Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Each week is judged by the goals in effect that week, so changing a goal doesn't rewrite past weeks.

| Event | XP |
|---|---|
| Each muscle's weekly sets goal met | +20 |
| Every muscle goal met | +100 |
| Weekly cardio time goal met | +75 |
| Weekly distance goal met | +75 |
| Workout-days goal met (set in Goals, default 4 days) | +50 |
| Streak: days goal met several weeks in a row | +20 per extra week, up to +200 |
| Each workout day missed in a finished week | −40, and the streak resets |

Your first week is never penalised. Total XP never goes below 0, but penalties can take you down a level.

The level card shows this week's workout days, what you'll earn by hitting your goal, what you'll lose if you fall short, and last week's result.

The **History** tab has an XP chart for the selected range:

- one bar per day for a week or month, per week for a year, and per month beyond that
- workout XP in green, weekly bonuses stacked on top, and missed-day penalties below the zero line
- your total for the range and your best day or week

Tap a bar to list where its XP came from: today's bar is listed to start with. The list shows:

- each workout, broken down into the 25 XP for logging it and each exercise's sets, or the cardio minutes. Notes appear when the 40-set or 180-minute caps apply.
- each weekly bonus, boss reward and missed-day penalty
- a total that matches the bar

Tap a workout in the list to open it. This week's bonuses appear on today's bar until the week ends.

Saving a workout shows the XP earned. Reaching a new level or title brings up a celebration. Tap the level card to see the title ladder and recent XP.

XP is worked out from your logged workouts, so editing or deleting a workout updates it. Example workouts don't earn XP.

## Body weight

Record your body weight at the top of **Goals**: type it in or use the − / + buttons (1 lb or 0.5 kg per tap). Tapping **Save goals** saves it with today's date.

- Changing it on a later day adds a new dated entry, so you build a history. Changing it again the same day updates that day's entry.
- Saving other goals without touching the weight doesn't add an entry.
- It isn't used for XP or goals.
- The **Stats** tab shows your current weight, the change over the last 30 days and since your first entry, and a line chart of every weigh-in. Tap a point to see its date and weight.
- "Record your body weight" is a step in the Get started checklist.

## Personal records

Forged keeps the heaviest set you've logged for each exercise. kg and lb are compared correctly.

- **While logging:** your best shows under the exercise name, for example "Your best: 185 lb × 5 · Oct 20".
- **When you beat it:** the save message says so, for example "New record: Bench Press 190 lb". The first time you log an exercise sets its record without announcing one.
- **On the Stats tab:** all your records are listed, newest first.

## Sharing your progress

Tap **Share progress** on the level card, or on the Level screen, to make two share images for this week. Swipe between them on a phone; wider screens show them side by side.

1. **Overview:** your level, title, XP bar and next title; this week's workouts, workout days, streak and total XP; the lifting heat map with your muscle goals (closest to done first) and any records you beat this week; and the title ladder.
2. **Gauges:** the cardio rings for time, distance and stairs; the four lifting gauges (sets, muscle goals, volume against last week, and push / pull); and your boss: the fight in progress, the boss waiting, or the next one to unlock. Each gauge shows whether you're on pace, and the white dot marks today's even-pace point.

**Share** sends both images through your phone's share menu. Where a browser can't share images, they're saved to your downloads instead. **Save images** saves both.

Everything is generated on your device; nothing is uploaded.

## Boss fights

Every 5 levels a **boss** appears. When you're ready, tap **Start fight**: you then have 24 hours, and every workout you log during the fight counts toward the challenge. The boss falls the moment you reach it. If time runs out, the boss stays and you can start again. You can also end a fight early.

Four types take turns, and each comes back harder:

| Type | Levels | Challenge within 24 hours |
|---|---|---|
| Runner | 5, 25, 45 ... | 10K, 15K, half marathon, 30K, marathon, 50K |
| Stairs | 10, 30, 50 ... | 150, 200, 275, 350, 425, 500 stories |
| Iron | 15, 35, 55 ... | 40, 45, 50, 55, 60, 70 lifting sets |
| Volume | 20, 40, 60 ... | 25k, 30k, 35k, 45k, 55k, 65k lb lifted |

Each reward equals the XP of the next two levels, plus 5% more for every boss before it. Rewards keep pace with the steeper level costs and keep growing: about 1,100 XP at level 5, 2,200 at 10, 14,650 at 50 and 39,200 at 100.

A boss beaten later on the same day it appeared counts too.

A new boss is announced with a popup. The crown **Boss** button in the header (and **Bosses** in the desktop sidebar) shows a badge while one is waiting. The Today tab shows the boss with a **Start fight** button, and during a fight, the time left and your progress.

## Leaderboard

A global leaderboard for signed-in users, with four tabs:

- **This week** and **This month:** all XP earned in the current week (Monday to Sunday) or month, the same total as the History page: workouts, weekly bonuses and boss rewards, minus any missed-day penalties. A countdown shows when the board resets.
- **Total XP:** all-time XP, including weekly bonuses and boss rewards.
- **Bosses:** bosses defeated.

**Each row shows:**
- the rank, with gold, silver and bronze for the top three
- the display name, title and title theme
- the level
- the score
- the workouts in that period, the weekly streak and bosses defeated

Your own row is highlighted, even if you're outside the top 100, and the top of the board shows your rank, for example "#2 of 42 players this week".

**Joining is opt-in.** Pick a display name (2–24 characters, unique). Others only see your name, title, level and scores, never your email or workouts. You can change the name or leave any time. Your entry updates itself as you train.

**Finding it:** a card on the Today tab shows your weekly rank. There's also a **Ranks** button in the header on wider screens and **Leaderboard** in the desktop sidebar. The Claude artifact version doesn't have a leaderboard, because it doesn't use Supabase.

The app reports your XP, matching what you see in Forged, and the database checks every number against what your logged workouts, weekly bonuses and boss fights in that period could earn. Anything above that is capped, and the level is recalculated, so the numbers can't be inflated by editing the app. The setup is in [`supabase/leaderboard.sql`](supabase/leaderboard.sql), run once in the SQL Editor after `schema.sql`.

## Understanding your progress

- **Get started checklist:** walks new users through setting goals, logging lifts and logging cardio. Each step ticks off when done. You can hide it.
- **Your progress:** shows how many muscle goals are met, how much of the cardio goal is done, the days left in the range, and the muscles furthest behind. Tap one to see it on the map.
- **Pace:** in the current week, month or year, each goal is marked *On pace*, *Behind pace* or *Goal met*. The comparison is with how far through the range you are; for example, 2 of 7 days in, you'd be on pace with 2/7 of the goal. The thin line on each bar marks that point. Past ranges show *Goal met* or *Missed*.
- **Legend:** each map labels its colour steps (none yet, under 25%, 25–49%, 50–74%, 75–99%, goal met). "How to read this map" explains how sets and minutes are counted.

## Cardio

The **Stats** tab also has a **Lifting** panel with four gauges:

- **Sets:** sets counted toward your muscle goals, out of your total weekly goal. Helper muscles count half, and sets beyond a muscle's goal don't add more.
- **Muscle goals:** a ring with one segment per muscle, coloured like the heat map. Tap a segment to see that muscle's numbers.
- **Volume:** total reps × weight against the previous week, month or year, with a note when you're up on last time.
- **Push / pull:** a dial comparing push sets (chest, shoulders, triceps) with pull sets (lats, traps, biceps). 40–60% push counts as balanced.

**Stairs** sessions are logged in stories climbed (− / + in steps of 5) instead of distance.

Tap **+ → Cardio** to log cardio: the date, then one or more activities, each with its type, minutes, optional distance (mi or km), or stories for stairs, and an optional name. Tap **+ Add activity** to log several at once, such as a run and a stair climb. Each activity is saved as its own session. If the day already has cardio, the form opens with that day's sessions and a new activity ready to fill in. You can change or remove any of them (✕) and save. The menu shows "Continue today's cardio" with the activities so far. Picking another date in the form, or using the Day view on an earlier date, does the same for that day. Editing a session from History changes just that session, and a new date moves it. Each activity maps to the muscles it works. For example, a cycle works quads fully and calves, hamstrings and glutes partly. Cardio progress shows as three doughnut gauges, **Time**, **Distance** and **Stairs**, each filling toward your weekly goal for the selected range (default 150 minutes, 10 miles and 50 stories a week, set in Goals). A white dot on each ring marks where you'd be today at an even pace.

## Running it

It's a single static file with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host it on any static host. For GitHub Pages: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root**.

Without an account, workouts are saved in the browser's `localStorage`, so they stay on that device and browser only. Sign in to sync them (below).

## Accounts and sync

Tap the person icon (**Sign in**) at the top right. Sign in with **Google**, or with your email: Forged emails you a sign-in link, and tapping it opens Forged signed in. Open the email on the device you want to sign in on. If you use Forged from your home screen, sign in with Google, because email links open in the browser instead. If the email ever contains a code (see the setup notes), tap **Email has a code instead?** and type it in.

Once you're signed in:

- Your workouts, goals (with their history), body weight, boss fights, title theme and units are stored in your account and sync to every device you sign in on. An open app picks up changes from another device within a few seconds, or when you switch back to it.
- Everything is also kept on the device. If you log a workout with no signal, it's saved and sent when you're back online. The account icon has a dot: green when synced, grey while syncing or waiting, red when offline or something needs attention. Tap it for details.
- If this browser already has workouts from before you signed in, Forged offers to add them to your account. Workouts already in the account aren't added twice.
- **Sign out** removes the account's copy from the device, and this browser's own workouts are shown again. If changes haven't synced yet, Forged warns you first.

Accounts use [Supabase](https://supabase.com) (sign-in and a Postgres database). The database's row-level security rules mean each person can only read and change their own rows, so the public key in the page is safe to share. The Claude artifact version keeps using its own built-in storage.

### Setting up Supabase

1. Create a Supabase project. Turn on the **Email** and **Google** sign-in providers, and set the **Site URL** and **Redirect URLs** to the GitHub Pages address.
2. The standard Supabase sign-in email works as it is. Optional: once you've set up custom SMTP (Supabase only lets you edit email templates with it), you can add `Your Forged code: {{ .Token }}` to the **Magic Link** template, so the email also includes a code.
3. Open the **SQL Editor**, paste [`supabase/schema.sql`](supabase/schema.sql) and tap **Run**. It creates the `workouts` and `profiles` tables, the security rules and live updates, and is safe to run again.
4. The project URL and publishable key are set at the top of the storage code in `index.html` (`const SB`).

## Backup and restore

At the bottom of **Goals**, **Export backup** saves a single `.json` file with your workouts, goals (including their history), body weight entries and settings (title theme, weight and distance units).

On another phone or browser, choose **Import backup** and pick that file. Forged shows what's in it, then lets you choose:

- **Merge into this device:** adds the backup's workouts and weigh-ins and keeps what's already there. The same workout is never added twice, and this device's goals and settings stay as they are.
- **Replace everything:** makes this device match the backup exactly, including goals and settings.

Nothing changes until you pick one.
